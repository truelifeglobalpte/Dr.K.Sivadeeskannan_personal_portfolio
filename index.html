About Dr. Sivadeeskannan

Advocate & Leader

Dr. K. Sivadeeskannan is a practicing Advocate enrolled with the Bar Council of Tamil Nadu & Puducherry (Enrolment No. MS.7787/2024), active across Chennai in civil, criminal, and political law. He holds a B.B.A., LL.B. (Hons) from Bharath Institute of Higher Education and Research, an MBA, an MBA in Human Resource Management from Alagappa University, and a Diploma in Social Service (D.SS.). He was conferred an Honorary Doctorate in Social Services in recognition of his community work.

Beyond litigation, he serves as Chief Executive Officer of True Life Global Pte. Ltd., a Singapore-based logistics, accounting, and business solutions company, and is the founder of Kumaresh Residency. He is also active in Tamil Nadu's public and political landscape, engaging regularly with state leadership on governance and youth-focused initiatives.
